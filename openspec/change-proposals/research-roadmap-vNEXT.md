## Three biggest gaps against the PRD

1. **FR-11: qualified, useful continuous self-learning.** The small energy
   head can update, but the fixed causal future-loss/retention study still
   lacks qualified execution. Repair only measured execution defects, then
   run the original scientific question without changing its estimand.
2. **FR-12: verification beyond local evidence and planted defects.** Surface
   invariance did not produce composition transfer. Test whether explicit
   evidence-set sufficiency handles missing hops and conflicts, while keeping
   the existing joint symbolic evaluator as a strong comparator. Natural
   extraction and an oracle-distinct verification advantage remain open.
3. **FR-05/FR-08/NFR-01: useful complete-service performance.** Feature reuse
   helps both languages equally. Measure the optimized path's remaining
   costs and bounded durable updates before proposing another kernel or
   accelerator. Numerical parity alone cannot meet the throughput requirement.

The active architecture is the hybrid generator plus energy verifier in
`ops/north-star.md`. The older PRD's generative aspiration does not reopen
the retired energy-as-generator experiments. The fixed FoVer publication
gate remains separate from these research questions.
