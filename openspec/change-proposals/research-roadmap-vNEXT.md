## Three Largest Gaps Against the PRD

1. **Credible source decisions (FR-12).** The stack has source projection,
   latent evidence views and calibrated heads, but no qualified current
   natural comparison after the source/protocol blocks. Qualify the repaired
   boundaries, then measure probability quality, decision cost, abstention
   and source sensitivity separately. These exposed development families
   cannot close the independent-generalization gap.
2. **Continuous constraint addition (FR-11).** Persistence and reweighting
   fixtures do not prove acquisition. Install newly admitted predicates from
   released feedback, test a later causal decision change against no-write,
   and compare future benefit/retention against a complete static bank.
   V683 tests a finite predicate lattice, not open-vocabulary discovery.
3. **Deployable and live-path evidence (FR-05/FR-08, ARC).** Component claims
   do not yet connect decision quality to full service cost or qualified
   accelerators. Measure the owned CPU service boundary, preserve actual
   ARC supervisor deltas and board custody, and retain the physical triggers
   needed for future device execution. Hardware acceleration and unseen ARC
   generalization remain open unless the evidence actually changes.
