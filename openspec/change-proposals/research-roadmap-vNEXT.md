## Three Largest Gaps to the PRD Vision

1. **The verified SOTA bank has no claim-grade selector result.** Carnot now
   has real proposals, token scores, exact outcomes, and family coverage, but
   no fresh support audit, matched-hardness diagnostic, held-source energy
   comparison, abstention curve, or cross-family shortcut audit.
2. **Continuous self-learning is still not demonstrated prospectively.** The
   earlier BCIT stream held only eight events over five groups. The PRD needs a
   sealed chronological stream, exact post-decision feedback, bounded memory,
   protected retention, and rollback under repeated updates.
3. **A learned energy has not crossed the portability boundary.** Carnot has
   neither an exact QUBO-to-Ising distribution receipt for the current
   entrance energy nor evidence that the scorer transfers across generator
   families without using model identity as a shortcut.

The live ARC causal-credit branch remains blocked by zero eligible hidden or
rotation units in Exp7072 and insufficient frozen evidence in Exp6921. V622
does not rerun that unchanged scope.

## Research Findings That Shape V622

- AgentCL (`arXiv:2606.02461`) shows that controlled reusable streams expose
  continual-learning behavior that naive streams hide. Exp7098 pairs reusable
  entrance rules with matched decoy events. Exp7099 compares persistent memory
  with equal-context and no-memory controls.
- ContinualSkillBench (`arXiv:2608.03874`) finds that explicit skills are not
  uniformly better than in-context adaptation. Exp7099 therefore cannot claim
  learning without beating equal-context controls on later protected groups.
- Solver-Hard (`arXiv:2607.17047`) shows that solver difficulty and model
  difficulty can diverge. Exp7094 constructs matched structural-difficulty
  cells before any selector result is interpreted.
- Extropic's Z1T report fixes the software target at degree 16 and separates
  stochastic fabric work from FPGA and dense readout work. Exp7097 reports
  exact algebra, finite distributions, routing overhead, and every exclusion.
  It makes no Z1, FPGA, power, or speed claim.
- The Energy to Say No and Energy-Based Action Heads work motivate selective
  risk at matched coverage. Exp7096 treats energy as an abstention score, not
  as proof. Exact reachability remains final authority.

The full source receipts and product checks are in `research-references.md`
under `V622 planner refresh - 2026-09-06`.

## Phase 1: Entrance Energy and Sparse Portability

Phase 1 consumes the completed Exp7086 bank. It first recovers the missing cold
audit. It then separates structural difficulty from model headroom, fits one
bounded energy, tests abstention, and proves software Ising portability.

### Exp7093 - Recovered cold entrance-bank sufficiency audit

**Purpose:** Produce the artifact Exp7087 failed to write.

**Method:** Reuse and promote the existing Exp7087 implementation. In a fresh
process, reparse raw outputs, recompute exact labels and witnesses, and attack
family deletion, source swaps, byte mutation, duplicate support, conflicts, and
future-label leakage. Run focused module coverage rather than a whole-repository
coverage denominator.

**Acceptance:** `entrance_support_audit_ready_score=1` requires authentic,
complete, conflict-free support. `entrance_selector_headroom_ready_score=1`
requires at least 30 held mixed-outcome units and no perfect non-oracle control.

**Gate:** None. Exp7086 is a fixed completed input, not a current-roadmap gate.

**Prior failures:** Exp7087 wrote no artifact after two attempts; Exp7081 had no
usable bank. The completed Exp7086 bank and the passing focused test module are
the changed prerequisites.

### Exp7094 - Matched-hardness entrance difficulty diagnostic

**Purpose:** Test whether structural difficulty is a valid proxy for proposal
headroom before interpreting an energy result.

**Method:** Freeze prompt-visible structural features and exact solver cost
features. Form matched pairs and strata with similar solver difficulty but
different model outcomes where the data supports them. Report all three model
families, all source groups, overlap, balance, and null cells.

**Acceptance:** `matched_difficulty_diagnostic_complete_score=1` requires every
predeclared stratum and diagnostic row. A null relationship is a complete null,
not a failed task.

**Gate:** `exp7093-recovered-entrance-bank-sufficiency-audit.entrance_support_audit_ready_score == 1`

**Prior failures:** None. Exp5868 is a successful SAT fixture used as a method
reference, not a failed scope to rerun.

### Exp7095 - Entrance energy versus matched strong controls

**Purpose:** Test whether one explicit bounded entrance energy improves held
selection over strong non-oracle controls.

**Method:** Fit on calibration source groups only. Compare energy with MRV,
target log probability, average and summed log likelihood, frequency, uniform
legal choice, a family-local scorer, and shuffled labels. Stratify the result by
the Exp7094 difficulty cells.

**Acceptance:** `entrance_energy_comparison_complete_score=1` means all arms
finished. `entrance_energy_value_ready_score=1` additionally requires at least
a five-point exact-success gain over the strongest non-oracle control, a paired
95 percent lower bound above zero, and no required-family regression.

**Gates:**

- `exp7093-recovered-entrance-bank-sufficiency-audit.entrance_support_audit_ready_score == 1`
- `exp7093-recovered-entrance-bank-sufficiency-audit.entrance_selector_headroom_ready_score == 1`
- `exp7094-matched-hardness-entrance-diagnostic.matched_difficulty_diagnostic_complete_score == 1`

**Prior failures:** Exp1006 was gate-blocked. Exp7067, Exp7082, and Exp7088 lost
their upstream chains. V622 consumes the completed bank through a recovered
cold audit and a current-roadmap hardness diagnostic.

### Exp7096 - Cold entrance-energy abstention and shift audit

**Purpose:** Determine whether the frozen energy reduces selective risk under
held shifts at matched coverage.

**Method:** Reconstruct the scorer in a fresh process. Freeze thresholds on
calibration groups. Compare energy, likelihood, structural, seeded random, and
no-abstention arms over source swaps, family deletion, conflict injection, and
numeric shifts.

**Acceptance:** `entrance_abstention_audit_complete_score=1` means every arm and
shift finished. `entrance_abstention_value_ready_score=1` also requires lower
selective risk than the strongest control at matched coverage with a paired 95
percent lower bound above zero and no negative family direction.

**Gate:** `exp7095-entrance-energy-matched-controls.entrance_energy_comparison_complete_score == 1`

**Prior failures:** Exp533 found no violation reduction for token-level cold
guidance. V622 tests post-bank abstention with frozen thresholds instead of
token steering.

### Exp7097 - Z1T-style degree-16 Ising and sampler receipt

**Purpose:** Prove that the frozen entrance energy has a lossless software path
to QUBO, Ising, finite sampling, and a degree-16 parent graph.

**Method:** Exhaustively verify affine energy, pairwise rank, and tie parity.
Compare exact Boltzmann probabilities with CPU Gibbs and parallel tempering at
three or more temperatures. Report schedules, autocorrelation, effective sample
size, routing, auxiliaries, quantization, and excluded costs.

**Acceptance:** `entrance_ising_parity_ready_score=1` requires exact algebra and
finite-distribution agreement. `degree16_software_mapping_ready_score=1`
requires explicit placement overhead and reconstruction parity.

**Gate:** `exp7095-entrance-energy-matched-controls.entrance_energy_comparison_complete_score == 1`

**Prior failures:** Exp7073, Exp7074, Exp7083, and Exp7090 were all blocked by
retired upstream chains. V622 uses one current completed energy producer and
keeps translation, sampling, and placement in one task.

## Phase 2: Prospective Continuous Self-Learning

This phase is independent of Phase 1 after reading the fixed Exp7064 and
Exp7086 inputs. It satisfies the continuous self-learning requirement without
changing model weights.

### Exp7098 - Controlled reusable-versus-decoy entrance stream

**Purpose:** Build enough sealed chronological evidence for a prospective
continuous-learning comparison.

**Method:** Create at least 144 unique events across all 12 source groups. Pair
reusable structural relations with matched decoy events. Freeze features and
the next decision before opening each exact outcome. Mark answer-only guesses,
hash-chain events, and reserve protected retention slices.

**Acceptance:** `controlled_causal_stream_ready_score=1` requires the full event
and group floors, reusable and decoy cells, zero future-field leakage, protected
slices, and byte-stable replay.

**Gate:** None.

**Prior failures:** Exp7070 had only eight events over five groups. V622 derives
the larger controlled stream from the completed 96-unit fixture and bank.

### Exp7099 - Verifier-signed continual constraint-memory A/B

**Purpose:** Test continuous self-learning from exact post-decision feedback in
bounded external memory.

**Method:** Compare context-bound signed updates, unsigned context-bound reuse,
flat reuse, validate-all, equal-context without persistent memory, and no reuse.
Use equal memory, context, validation, and decision budgets. Admit, reverse, or
disable rules only from prior exact outcomes.

**Acceptance:** `continual_memory_comparison_complete_score=1` means every arm
and chronological slice finished. `continual_memory_value_ready_score=1`
requires positive time-averaged gain over the strongest control, no protected-
slice regression, fewer harmful transfers, and stable direction across memory
capacities.

**Gate:** `exp7098-controlled-causal-entrance-stream.controlled_causal_stream_ready_score == 1`

**Prior failures:** Exp6978 was a complete null and Exp7070 was blocked by an
insufficient stream. V622 adds controlled reusable and decoy relations,
equal-context controls, and the full evidence floor.

### Exp7100 - Cold continual-memory safety and rollback audit

**Purpose:** Verify that any learned memory effect is causal, durable, bounded,
and recoverable outside the producer process.

**Method:** Recompute the full trajectory from immutable inputs. Attack context
drift, guess poisoning, reordered outcomes, partial writes, stale parents,
capacity pressure, crash recovery, and rollback at multiple evaluation times.

**Acceptance:** `continual_memory_cold_audit_ready_score=1` requires byte-stable
replay, atomic transactions, rejected guess poison, zero protected regression,
successful rollback, and row/headline agreement.

**Gate:** `exp7099-verifier-signed-continual-memory-ab.continual_memory_comparison_complete_score == 1`

**Prior failures:** Exp6979 was a complete null and Exp7071 was gate-blocked.
V622 audits a larger controlled stream and a completed current-roadmap A/B.

## Shared Evidence Rules

Every task must emit `field_principles`, `preconditions_checked`, `duration_s`,
`source_artifact_hashes`, per-unit rows, `random_seed`,
`reproducibility_checksum`, `gate_check_summary`, `verifier_is_oracle`, the
free-text `honest_verdict`, and the closed `verdict_class`. Every task also
emits a prose `inference_substrate`, a closed `inference_substrate_class`, and
`execution_venue`.

The compute classes are `aggregation`, `no_model_load`,
`model_load_no_generation`, `model_bounded_generation`,
`model_full_generation`, and `blocked_no_run`. The V622 execution venue is
`host`. A task blocked by an unchanged external condition uses `blocked`, not
`partial`. `partial` is reserved for the task's own retryable incompleteness.

Every comparison emits one row for every unit, arm, fold, seed, source group,
time slice, capacity, or condition used in a headline. Exact outcomes may
evaluate a held decision only after the decision is frozen. A positive learned
claim must keep `verifier_is_oracle=false`; oracle-only algebra tasks use
`circular_positive` when their class is positive.

## Milestone Completion Rule

V622 is complete when all 12 YAML tasks have terminal dispositions and Exp7102
has independently described every slot. Science success remains branch-local:

- authentic support does not prove energy value;
- a complete energy comparison may honestly be null;
- lower selective risk is not exact proof;
- a host software placement is not Z1 or FPGA execution;
- external-memory gain is not model-weight learning;
- cross-family transfer is not live deployment.

The roadmap must not be shortened without changing this document and
`research-roadmap-next.yaml` together.
