## 1. What milestone 2026.08.550 proved

Milestone 2026.08.550 converted the V549 all-family transport null into two
narrow, usable research paths.

### Factor-learning path

- Exp6394 froze one development-selected harness per mandated GGUF family
  before held access.
- Exp6395 issued four exact, hash-bound capability licenses: Gemma-4-31B on
  `threshold_guard` and `route_guard`; Gemma-4-26B-A4B on `route_guard` and
  `conservation_guard`. Every Qwen3.6 cell abstained. Two other Gemma cells
  were rejected as underpowered. Universal support is false.
- Exp6396 improved untouched future exact yield from 0.50 to 0.6667 inside
  licensed cells only.
- Exp6397 committed two factors through predecessor-bound atomic transactions
  and improved future exact yield by 0.25 without measured retention harm.
- Exp6398 reproduced a 0.25 gain in a default-off future consumer and kept
  production disabled.
- Exp6399 found no unsafe license or transaction behavior, but returned
  `complete_null`: the audit scope included the already flagged Exp6385 input,
  so broad or public factor promotion remained blocked.

### ARC path

- Exp6393 repaired the nested numeric gate at the producer boundary.
- Exp6400 proved that the active-goal mechanism is reachable and fires on the
  normal live path while remaining a non-intervening shadow.
- Exp6401 ran a fresh causal holdout. The active treatment fired 24 times,
  improved the exact progress proxy by 3.5, and reduced false accepts by nine.
- Exp6402 found no critical provenance or oracle-timing violation. It correctly
  kept public ARC eligibility false because the evidence was an internal route
  test, not a solve, policy promotion, or registry entry.

### Terminal boundary

Exp6403 therefore reported partial factor capability, partial positive FR11
evidence, a positive internal ARC route, no solve claim, unchanged hardware,
and no public claim eligibility. These are the starting facts for V551.

## 2. The three largest gaps to the PRD vision

1. **The factor result is narrow and its independent claim boundary is not
   clean.** The four licensed cells are useful, but the V550 audit mixed a
   previously flagged V549 artifact into the public decision surface. Carnot
   needs a fresh, powered, V550-and-later-only replication with a clean
   evidence ledger. It must not retry universal support.

2. **Continuous self-learning is short-horizon and lacks governed memory under
   drift.** V550 showed two commits over one bounded chronological stream. The
   PRD calls for ongoing improvement without forgetting. Carnot has not yet
   tested multi-session contamination, license expiry, source supersession,
   local graph verification, raw-ledger escalation, bounded growth, or restart
   recovery under distribution shift.

3. **The ARC mechanism has not controlled executed actions in a fresh opt-in
   policy trial.** V550 established shadow reachability and a positive causal
   progress proxy, but production promotion stayed at zero. The next valid step
   is a default-disabled, explicitly opt-in experimental policy A/B on fresh
   live windows, followed by a disjoint held-game-family replication. It is not
   a game or level solve task.

## 3. New research inputs

The V551 source refresh is recorded in `research-references.md` before this
roadmap was designed.

- **GRACE** (arXiv:2607.09175) motivates a typed graph and verification limited
  to the affected neighborhood of each update under distribution shift.
- **ConsistencyGate** (arXiv:2607.22962) isolates compounding write-time memory
  contamination and motivates a controlled corruption stream.
- **TierMem** (arXiv:2602.17913; ICLR 2026 MemAgents) motivates a fast compiled
  summary tier linked to an immutable raw-evidence tier with sufficiency-based
  escalation.
- **A-MAC** (arXiv:2603.04549; ICLR 2026 MemAgents) supplies interpretable
  admission features. Carnot uses them as diagnostics only; exact checkers,
  source hashes, predecessor freshness, and release receipts remain authority.

The refresh found no new authenticated TSU route, public Kona implementation,
changed FPGA board receipt, or KAN result that reopens a closed lane.

## 6. Phase 1 — Provenance-linked factor memory

### Exp6407 — Typed two-tier memory and contamination protocol

Implement and freeze a research substrate with an immutable raw event/release
ledger, a compiled typed factor graph, raw-source hashes, affected-neighborhood
receipts, exact predecessor heads, expiry and supersession records, and
fail-closed raw-tier escalation. Freeze a controlled stream containing supported,
contradicted, implicit, stale, duplicated, superseded, and poisoned candidate
events. No LLM runs in this experiment.

### Exp6408 — Fresh powered write-time admission A/B

Gated on Exp6407 readiness. Run the four Exp6395 licensed Gemma cells on fresh
held events. Compare a sandboxed write-everything arm with provenance-plus-exact
admission. Qwen and rejected Gemma cells must abstain. Measure contamination
propagation, exact future yield, false accepts, escalation, and cost. The arm may
write only to isolated experimental heads.

## 7. Phase 2 — Continuous self-learning under drift

### Exp6409 — Multi-session graph-local continuous self-learning

This is the required continuous self-learning experiment. It is gated on a
ready, positive Exp6408 result. Run at least 72 chronological events across four
sessions, three drift regimes, four process restarts, and multiple expiry or
supersession boundaries. Compare frozen, flat transactional, and graph-local
two-tier learners at matched work. Evaluate future events once. Commits require
exact support; contamination, forgetting, and harmful transfer must remain zero
or fail closed.

### Exp6410 — Default-off tiered consumer across restarts

Gated on Exp6409 readiness and positive future exact-yield delta. Freeze all
writes and evaluate at least 36 untouched future events across restart
boundaries. Compare raw-only, compiled-tier, and frozen baselines. Measure exact
agreement, raw escalation, latency, bounded growth, abstention, and stale-cache
rejection. Production enable count must stay zero.

### Exp6411 — Independent factor-memory safety audit

Run regardless of upstream gates. Do not invoke an LLM or synthesize missing
cells. Attack provenance, contamination, graph locality, expiry, supersession,
raw escalation, transaction atomicity, restart recovery, license scope, pooled
partial cells, and claim laundering. The audit uses the clean Exp6406 boundary.

## 8. Phase 3 — Opt-in ARC policy evidence and reconciliation

### Exp6412 — Fresh opt-in active-goal executed-policy A/B

Gated on the positive Exp6401 causal receipt. Keep the production route off.
Within an explicit experimental arm, allow the active-goal route to affect the
executed legal action after the action is frozen and before the outcome is read.
Use at least ten fresh live windows and 60 visible transitions. Compare route-off
and route-on at matched work. Make no solve or registry claim.

### Exp6413 — Held-game-family policy replication

Gated on Exp6412 readiness, positive progress delta, and non-increased false
accepts. Use a disjoint held game family, fresh windows, matched budgets, and the
same frozen policy. Report family heterogeneity and missing cells. A positive
result may support an internal opt-in route candidate only; it is not production
promotion and not a solve.

### Exp6414 — Independent ARC executed-policy safety audit

Run regardless of gates. Verify live-agent provenance, action/outcome ordering,
window and family disjointness, legal-action equality, treatment firing, hidden
source/search/adapter counts, default-off production state, solve wording, and
registry immutability. Do not run new ARC attempts.

### Exp6415 — V551 adversarial capstone and PRD-gap reconciliation

Recompute every gate and claim from terminal artifacts. Reconcile the clean
factor evidence boundary, governed multi-session FR11 result, held consumer,
opt-in ARC policy evidence, audits, model/GPU receipts, and unchanged hardware
state. Update only applicable specs and ops documents with clean evidence.

## 13. Expected milestone decision

V551 succeeds scientifically if it can answer two questions with clean terminal
evidence:

1. Can Carnot keep improving a licensed factor system across sessions and drift
   while exact provenance, bounded memory, rollback, and abstention prevent
   contamination and forgetting?
2. Does the active-goal route improve executed live ARC policy progress on
   fresh and held-family windows without false-accept growth, oracle leakage, or
   a solve claim?

A null answer is valid. The capstone must preserve it rather than expanding the
claim surface.
