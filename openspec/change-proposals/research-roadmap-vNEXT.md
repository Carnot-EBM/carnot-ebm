## Thesis

Milestone `.519` established a useful exact-event substrate. Exp5825 produced a clean canonical
adaptive-memory contract; Exp5826 produced a deterministic 360-row, four-family chronological
out-of-template stream; and Exp5827 cleanly credited active minimal-core structural acquisition
over the matched-template control. Those three artifacts pass the live adversarial verifier.

The rest of the claimed lifecycle is not yet headline evidence. Exp5828 reports strong
future-validated lift, exact protected-prefix retention, zero unsafe updates, exact rollback, and
restart equivalence, but its checked-in artifact is stamped `flagged_adversarial=true` with a
CRITICAL duration/substrate mismatch and a methodology warning. Exp5829 reports positive
signature-compatible replay, but it depends on the flagged Exp5828 artifact. The current milestone
is terminal, but terminal does not mean every artifact is eligible for promotion.

Milestone `.520` therefore begins with an independent evidence qualification. It recomputes
Exp5825-Exp5829 row-level claims from immutable source rows and implementation code, distinguishes
the clean exact-stream/structural-acquisition branch from the provisional lifecycle/replay branch,
adds shortcut and perturbation controls, and emits separate bare qualification scalars. Downstream
tasks use structured gates rather than narrative optimism.

The principal new science tests a materially different internal-representation mechanism. Exp5200
was an absolute final-layer probe on one older Gemma/MMLU-Pro surface and did not beat tuned
self-consistency or three zero-training controls. CORTEX and CrossHallu suggest two missing axes:
comparative counterfactual representations and explicit cross-domain alignment. `.520` builds
exact constraint-present/constraint-ablated and correct/incorrect causal pairs, extracts
output-free embeddings from all three mandated current SOTA GGUF families, and evaluates
model-family- and constraint-family-held-out logistic, MLP, and compact KAN energy controls. Exact
solvers remain labels and release authority. A clean null retires this bounded final-layer route.

Continuous self-learning remains mandatory. If the lifecycle evidence qualifies, `.520` reduces
exact-oracle availability and tests whether versioned external state can choose queries, buffer
rejected updates, consolidate slowly, and preserve prospective lift, retention, safety, rollback,
and bounded memory. GGUF/model weights remain immutable. This is the SkillOpt-like external-state
pattern applied to exact constraints, not uncontrolled self-editing.

The live ARC branch resumes only on a preserved open surface: agent-owned world feedback. A bounded
write-protected fact tape stores the live agent's own legal actions and exact observations. A
game-blind probe scheduler is then compared with budget-matched random, periodic, and current-policy
controls on held-out games/actions. There is no public-game solve target, source inspection,
offline BFS, hand GameAdapter, per-game model, generated-answer CEGIS, or registry credit.

The milestone makes four falsifiable claims:

1. `.519` lifecycle evidence can be independently replayed and separated into promotion-eligible
   and provisional classes without mutating historical artifacts.
2. Paired counterfactual current-GGUF embeddings contain model-family-portable constraint energy
   signal beyond absolute-state, cosine, and simple classifier controls; otherwise this route is
   retired.
3. Future-validated adaptive memory can retain benefit and safety under a substantially reduced
   exact-query budget; otherwise the sparse-oracle extension is retired while full-oracle evidence
   is preserved.
4. A write-protected fact tape plus outcome-driven evidence acquisition improves held-out live E3
   transition or policy behavior under an identical legal-action budget; no level solve is needed.

## The three biggest gaps to the PRD vision

### Gap 1 — FR-11 lifecycle evidence is synthetic and partly adversarially flagged

Carnot has clean structural acquisition but no promotion-eligible proof that prospective
quarantine/promotion/rollback and selective replay survive an independent reconstruction. The
reported performance is unusually high and fast, making target construction, shortcut features,
metric recomputation, chronology, and substrate classification first-class questions. Even a clean
full-oracle result would still leave continuous learning dependent on exact feedback for every
candidate. `.520` first qualifies the evidence, then tests a sparse exact-oracle regime.

### Gap 2 — FR-08/FR-09 still lack an oracle-distinct, portable current-GGUF energy signal

The frozen FoVer result remains the defensible production baseline, and the generated-text/logprob
PHASE D lane is retired. The only local final-layer experiment, Exp5200, was a negative absolute
probe on one older model/benchmark pairing. Carnot has not tested causal comparative differences,
constraint ablation, all three mandated 2026 GGUF families, model-family holdout, constraint-family
holdout, or alignment-aware transfer. Without that evidence, the internal-energy layer is not a
portable learned verifier.

### Gap 3 — Learned state is not live-path reachable or hardware-portable

The live E3 agent still lacks a deterministic write-protected record of its own world evidence and
a budgeted policy for acquiring missing facts. The accepted exact-memory operations also lack a
small bounded Python/Rust ABI that can be mapped honestly to attached boards. `.520` separates
these concerns: live ARC tests agent-owned observations; the microkernel tests deterministic
state-operation parity; hardware work records only authenticated capability/precondition receipts.

## Success, falsification, and retirement rules

- Exp5839 never rewrites Exp5828. A qualification scalar is 1.0 only when independent
  recomputation, shortcut controls, provenance, and the live adversarial verifier all pass.
- Exp5842 is a bounded reattempt after Exp5200. Credit requires a positive paired lower bound over
  the strongest preregistered control on headroom-present held-model and held-constraint cells.
  Same-verdict failure retires this final-layer comparative route.
- Exp5843 is the required continuous-self-learning experiment. It changes external versioned state,
  never GGUF weights. Credit requires prospective lift, at least 50% exact-query reduction, exact
  retention/rollback/restart, zero unsafe accepts, no family harm, and bounded memory.
- Exp5845 is credited only through live-agent self-discovery. It cannot claim a public solve,
  offline proxy solve, outer-loop RE solve, or registry update.
- Exp5846 may claim parity and bounded useful work only. Exp5847 may claim only capability mapping
  and authenticated command receipts. Neither may infer acceleration from simulation or topology.
- Exp5848 always runs, excludes flagged artifacts from headlines, preserves nulls and blockers,
  applies retirement mechanically, and never changes the frozen FoVer 0.9131 result without new
  exact benchmark evidence.
