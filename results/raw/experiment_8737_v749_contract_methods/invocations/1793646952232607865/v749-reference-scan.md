### Promising findings and decisions

- [RT4CHART, 2603.27752v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27752v2), revised July 20,
  separates entailed, contradicted and baseless claims, with answer spans and
  context evidence. The revised abstract attributes most benefit to local claim
  processing and selective benefit to global verification. Test local versus
  joint evidence on constructed composition defects and valid alternatives;
  do not equate a missing premise with contradiction or claim a reproduction.
- [VerifierBench, 2610.28679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28679), October 19,
  tests valid alternatives as well as invalid outputs. Carnot's equal-call
  result is now positive on its exposed constructed worlds. The next useful
  question is transfer to defect families absent from probe design, with a
  separately implemented truth evaluator and explicit false-rejection rows.
- [Capacity-Constrained Online Convex Optimization with Delayed Feedback,
  2606.11711](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11711), June 10, models finite pending
  feedback and online delay expiration. Record admission, lost feedback and
  realized delay in the existing fixed causal study. Do not change its learner
  or transfer convex regret guarantees to Carnot's nonlinear Brier head.
- [HSRM, 2608.30841](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30841), August 31, reuses frozen
  generator representations for a small trained verifier. File for later:
  Carnot lacks qualified current generator execution and hidden-state access.
  Its resource-reuse idea is consistent with removing measured duplicate
  feature extraction, but that optimization is not an HSRM implementation.
- [On-chip KAN learning, 2602.02056v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02056v4),
  revised June 19, motivates sparse fixed-point updates. Local resident parity
  exists; per-feedback persistence remains expensive. Measure bounded lifecycle
  windows at identical durability, including renormalization and restart.
  Kernel latency cannot stand in for a complete online-learning service.
