# V750 ARC horizon sensitivity

Exp8760 tests whether the closed cross-game supervisor conclusion survives
missing follow-up and unequal arm overlap. It reads Exp8746's qualified sealed
frontier and registry before any new receipt. Empty deltas trigger no live run.
The original sixteen episodes, four games, eight pairs and 120-action horizon
remain closed. Historical producer failures and quarantines remain unchanged.

The preregistered grid crosses five game masks (none or one of four games),
four follow-up cutoffs (120, 30, 60, 90) and five arm masks (none or one of four
arms): 100 cases. Each case uses private primitive copies and the unchanged
action/observer reducer. A direct independent reconstruction reads primitive
status and count fields. Agreement is required for every episode in every case.

For each arm the denominator is the full intended episode roster. An observed
positive contributes [1,1], an observed complete negative contributes [0,0],
and any missing, censored or unavailable arm outcome contributes [0,1].
Arm bounds are sums of these intervals divided by the original roster size.
The contrast A minus B is [lower(A)-upper(B), upper(A)-lower(B)]. Each game/arm
cell is retained. Outcome removal must widen or preserve every effect interval.
Complete-case means are secondary diagnostics. No random-censoring assumption
or invented successful observation enters the bound.

Shared-arm admission requires at least three overlapping games and five
observed firings per shared game/arm cell. These are rejection rules, not proof
of generalization. Recorder on/off observations and seeds do not multiply the
four independent games. Selected/applied status, reset cause, follow-up horizon
and unresolved events remain separate.

The sealed source has sixteen censored outcomes, zero complete outcomes and
only the exploration-diversity arm represented. Expected arm bounds are [0,1]
and every arm contrast is [-1,1]. All perturbations must retain the conclusion
that benefit is unidentified. This is an exposed-development method diagnostic;
it does not establish causal supervisor benefit, transfer or a credited solve.

Scoped validation retains exact argv, exit codes, JUnit, actual parent/child
coverage SQLite and full log hashes. E2E-017, E2E-023 and private E2E-021 run
with their original assertions. Repository-wide health remains a separate
bounded diagnostic. Cold replay reconstructs the grid from sealed primitives
and rejects rehashed report changes. Unchanged terminal validators precede
atomic publication. No model, game, policy change or numerical training is used.
