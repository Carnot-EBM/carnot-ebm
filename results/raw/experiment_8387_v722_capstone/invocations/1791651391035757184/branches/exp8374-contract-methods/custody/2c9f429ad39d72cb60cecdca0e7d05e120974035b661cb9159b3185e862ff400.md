## 2026-10-10 — V722 planning scan: direct learning services and independent evidence

This entry precedes V722 experiment design. The scan covers all eight requested
arXiv topics and all six secondary channels. Rechecked sources are marked as
such. External results are not Carnot measurements. The V721 artifact review
finds qualified utility nulls and an unresolved numerical sigmoid contract.

### Findings selected for methods work

- **Online KAN, rechecked:** Hoang, Gupta and Harris,
  [2602.02056v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02056v4), revised June 19, 2026.
  Compact spline support supports sparse online writes. Measure recovery and
  complete transaction cost around the existing direct evaluator. This does
  not require certifying an approximate sigmoid or repeating a utility claim.
- **KANELÉ, rechecked:** the same authors,
  [2512.12850v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12850v3), revised June 16, 2026.
  Lookup deployment motivates a separate numerical research lane. Carnot's
  spline bound is already derived, but an empirical libm allowance is not a
  universal proof. A separately versioned logit decision policy could remove
  the transcendental operation from action selection. It must not silently
  replace the old probability policy or revise the closed H1/H2 results.
- **Independent evaluation, rechecked:** Li et al.,
  [OpenHalDet, 2606.06959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06959), June 5, 2026,
  separates generation, signals, labels and scoring. Its
  [author repository](https://github.com/Nellie179/Hallucination-Detection)
  describes model-generated truthfulness labels and independently licensed
  source datasets. A repository is not proof of a downloadable, independently
  labeled candidate corpus. Inspect actual released operands, lineage and
  costs before designing a new semantic benchmark. Do not execute its default
  model or paid annotation pipeline.
- **Evidence sufficiency, rechecked:** Kumar,
  [2608.00585](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00585), August 1, 2026, distinguishes
  single-chunk verification from multi-hop evidence sufficiency. Its
  [released traces](https://github.com/iamhero2709/verification-without-sufficiency)
  provide a second feasibility lead. Gold decomposition is an oracle ceiling;
  it cannot be fed to a deployed selector as if the agent discovered it.
- **Finite feedback, rechecked:** Ryabchenko, Attias and Roy,
  [2606.11711](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11711), June 2026, studies delayed
  feedback with limited tracking. Preserve causal issue/release order and
  explicit lost-feedback rows in a durable learner. No new scheduler sweep or
  borrowed regret guarantee follows from this reference.

### Recent updates and additional primary topics

- **New scan finding:** Betteti, Lahijanian and Laurenti,
  [Safe-by-Design Learning via Energy-based Neural Networks, 2609.36942v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36942v2),
  revised September 30, 2026. Hopfield/port-Hamiltonian dynamics produce
  invariant sets under stated assumptions. Defer adoption: continuous-state
  dynamical safety is different from sentence truth and from SciPy rounding.
- **New scan finding:** Extropic's October 1, 2026
  [First Sparks of Thermodynamic Recursive Intelligence](https://extropic.ai/writing/baby-thermo-rsi/)
  describes execution-plus-rubric evaluation for research agents. Its linked
  [hinton-problems repository](https://github.com/cybertronai/hinton-problems)
  supplies reproducible reference tasks. Adopt the separation of execution
  correctness from task benefit. Defer generator post-training and synthetic
  benchmark expansion. The post's planned 2027 hardware loop is not local TSU
  availability or a Carnot speed measurement.
- **EBM reasoning:** [EBT, 2507.02092](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02092), July
  2025, and [ARM–EBM, 2512.15605v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15605v4), revised
  May 25, 2026, remain architectural context. Likelihood compatibility and an
  ARM–EBM correspondence do not certify factual correctness. Defer foundation
  model training and the retired external-text ranker construction.
- **Neural constraints:**
  [Certified Correctness in Neural Constraint Reasoning, 2608.14569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14569)
  supports explicit symbolic checks. The search index reports inconsistent
  dates for this ID; do not treat that metadata as a new October result.
- **Energy-guided generation:**
  [Primal-Dual Guided Decoding, 2605.09749](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09749)
  targets discrete diffusion. [Draft-Conditioned Constrained Decoding,
  2603.03305](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03305) separates drafting and validity
  enforcement. Both are deferred while current local CUDA qualification is
  blocked. Neither licenses replacing the mandated Qwen3.8-27B generator.
- **Ising and hardware:**
  [FPGA decomposition, 2602.15985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15985) motivates
  including host preparation and transfer costs. Its custom solver does not
  supply a KV260 spline kernel. [Ising model compression, 2503.20123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20123)
  is a separate feature-selection direction; defer without a matching workload.
