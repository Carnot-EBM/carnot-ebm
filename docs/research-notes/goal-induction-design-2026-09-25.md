# Goal induction when the first win has not been seen

Research and design only. No production path changed. The scored agent first induces `engine` and `is_level_complete` from a stall window with no level-up (`python/carnot/agentic/arc_executable_world_model.py:8910-8975`, `python/carnot/agentic/arc_competition_agent.py:8695-8726`). The environment exposes level progress only after an action (`python/carnot/agentic/arc_agi3_live_adapter.py:352-353`). A sequence of non-wins rules out some hypotheses. It cannot identify a unique win rule.

## 1. Measured problem

The earlier registered-route check covered **38 agent-written sources**: eight completed think-ON responses and 30 codeonly responses. Only sp80 think-ON was false on every pre-win route frame and true on the level-up frame. Its score did not improve before the final frame. No source supplied a validated pre-win goal gradient (`docs/research-notes/goal-gradient-design-2026-09-25.md:66-122`; `results/raw/goal_gradient_induced_check_2026_09_25/per_engine.json:1`). The two timed-out think-ON windows have no source and are outside 38 (`docs/research-notes/b2-think-on-pilot-2026-09-24.md:14-35`). Route correctness is limited evidence. A separate source-derived sp80 predicate fired on non-winning game-over frames during random play (`docs/research-notes/gate-usefulness-2026-09-24.md:409-415`).

Goal errors can survive good dynamics. In Experiment 10012, su15 codeonly and ft09 think-ON predicted many real transitions but declared a non-winning real state complete (`docs/research-notes/gate-usefulness-2026-09-24.md:60-69`). Transition accuracy alone did not separate useful engines; the main exact gate accepted no useful induced model (`docs/research-notes/gate-usefulness-2026-09-24.md:45-69`, `results/experiment_10012_gate_usefulness.json:4019`). The live goal-consistency veto has no positive event in these stall windows (`python/carnot/agentic/arc_llm_reinduction.py:2205-2220`, `python/carnot/agentic/arc_llm_reinduction.py:2290-2313`). Earlier induced dynamics were often poor too: the codeonly mean masked change fidelity was 0.128, and think-ON improved it to 0.574 without clearing the exact live gate (`docs/research-notes/b2-think-on-pilot-2026-09-24.md:24-63`). Correct-dynamics controls are therefore needed before blaming only the goal.

## 4. First real win and transfer check

I replayed all recorded Experiment 10009 actions in the offline simulator. I read `frame.levels_completed`, not the harness's broken `peak_level` field (`python/carnot/experiment_7431_v651_arc_live_sentinel.py:1380-1384`, `docs/research-notes/b2-induction-failure-triage-2026-09-23.md:134-138`). All **39 started episodes** replayed without error. The remaining 105 of 144 were unstarted. Seven started episodes reached level one: sp80 in 3/3 and vc33 in 4/4. No episode reached level two. In the three common seeds per game, this reproduces the triage's 6/36 finding (`results/raw/goal_induction_design_2026_09_25/live_replay.json:1560-1658`; `docs/research-notes/b2-induction-failure-triage-2026-09-23.md:134-138`). This is a timed, incomplete sample, not a hidden-game first-win rate.

The two games with a live first level-up also have registered routes to the next level. I reused the Experiment 10012 route loader and first-level trajectory builder (`python/carnot/experiment_10012_gate_usefulness.py:1250-1304`). On each route, I took the **observed frame after L0→L1** as the lone positive and all prior L0 frames as negatives. I ran the existing single-positive count, richer-scalar, and relational energies, then treated energy zero as a firing predicate on the registered L1→L2 route (`python/carnot/agentic/arc_agi3_goal_induction.py:106-150`, `python/carnot/agentic/arc_agi3_goal_induction.py:190-224`, `python/carnot/agentic/arc_agi3_goal_induction.py:237-295`).

All six methods induced an energy and all six falsely fired on the next level's opening frame (`results/raw/goal_induction_design_2026_09_25/single_positive_transfer.json:4-237`). That result is forced by the observation format: the L0 “win frame” **is the same grid** as the L1 opening, and each method was trained to score that grid zero. The actual L0 terminal board may not be rendered before `next_level()` (`python/carnot/agentic/arc_solver_kit.py:22-32`). A correct transfer test needs a terminal-state representation grounded in the winning **transition**, not this post-transition grid. The six route results are a feasibility check on the currently observable exemplar, not a test of a hypothetical true terminal snapshot.

## 5. Outside work and candidate approaches

The closest research does not make an unseen win predicate identifiable from arbitrary self-play. [Plan recognition as planning](https://ijcai.org/Proceedings/09/Papers/296.pdf) infers goals from another actor's purposeful actions; this agent observes its own exploration, so that assumption is missing. [Self-directed action-model learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03485) uses exploratory plans without a given goal, but learns actions, not the hidden reward. [Width-based planning](https://nirlipo.github.io/publication/lipovetzky-2012-width/) and [Go-Explore](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.10995) motivate novelty and returnable frontier states for sparse first rewards. [Active reward-automaton inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15714) motivates selecting probes that distinguish reward hypotheses. Its reward observations are analogous to real level changes here. [Hindsight Experience Replay](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01495) learns from achieved goals, but it does not reveal an unobserved terminal board. These are method analogies, not evidence of ARC solve lift.

| Candidate | Hidden-game operation before a win | Cost and likely failure | Prior art |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Salience and dynamics hypothesis bank** | From visible changes, propose a bounded set: movable-object contact with fixed targets, region fill, color-count changes, and within-frame target matching. Keep several rules alive. A model-predicted success is a probe target, not a confirmed win. Reject a rule if it fires on a real no-level-up frame. | `O(grid cells + object pairs)` to extract features, plus bounded model calls to reach proposed states. HUD motion can look salient; wrong dynamics invent reachability. Negative frames alone leave most wrong rules alive. | [Exploratory action-model learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03485); [active reward-automaton inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15714). |
| **B. Novelty-led first-win search** | Prefer actions that create unseen object, relation, or action-effect facts. Revisit promising frontier states through known action traces. Check actual `levels_completed` after each action. Use no guessed predicate for termination. | One bounded feature scan per state and `O(seen facts)` memory. Irrelevant animation or HUD churn can dominate. Returning to frontier states can waste real actions; model drift can make stored traces fail. | [Width-based planning](https://nirlipo.github.io/publication/lipovetzky-2012-width/); [Go-Explore](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.10995). |
| **C. Transition-grounded transfer after a real level-up** | Bank the last action, before-grid, and post-level-up opening. Use a verified model only to propose a counterfactual terminal state or a causal guard. Test that rule against later real level changes. Do not call the post-level-up opening a win grid. | One extra bounded model reconstruction and feature comparison per win, then ordinary predicate checks. It cannot help level one. A wrong model or a changed next-level mechanic can invalidate transfer. | [Hindsight Experience Replay](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01495); [active reward-automaton inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15714). |

## 6. Recommendation and pre-registered test

Recommend a **bounded hypothesis-probe loop**: A supplies candidate destinations; B supplies a direction when hypotheses tie or none survives. Use the existing two-sided contract to keep unverified hypotheses from ending search. After a real level-up, test C separately. This follows the measured failure: 32 of 33 callable predicates were wrong on the registered route, while only two exposed a pre-win false positive. A negative-only veto would catch **2/32 wrong** and preserve the one route-correct predicate; **30/32 wrong** would remain (`results/raw/goal_induction_design_2026_09_25/negative_only_veto.json:1-13`). An observed positive event or a targeted contradiction is needed to do more.

Freeze the following experiment **before implementation or tuning**:

1. Use the ten Experiment 10010 stall windows, the 38 cached sources, and the Experiment 10013 `LIVE_SCORED` wrapper. Fix the hypothesis templates and their order from *visible stall-window frames only*. Limit the bank to 16 rules. Exclude any rule that fires on a recorded non-level-up frame. Keep all exclusions and unsupported cases in the artifact. The scorer may know the public simulator outcome; the proposer and action selector may not (`python/carnot/experiment_10013_planner_dedup_tiebreak.py:840-915`, `python/carnot/experiment_10012_gate_usefulness.py:30-35`).
2. Run paired arms from identical reset states. **Current** uses each cached induced engine and goal. **Probe** uses that same engine with the frozen hypothesis bank, one real action at a time, and real level-change feedback. **Dynamics control** substitutes the correct expert engine but keeps the same goal-blind probe decisions. **Search ceiling** uses the 10013 expert engine and expert goal. The expert goal never enters the Probe arms. Include all ten windows, including invalid sources as explicit failures.
3. Share **20,000 engine calls total** and **80 real actions** per game between replans. Keep the 10013 action mask, candidate vocabulary, reset logic, and real replay. Log each frozen hypothesis, predicted outcome, executed legal action, observed grid and level change, rejected hypothesis, and model/real divergence. A predicted win without a real level-up spends an action and is scored as a false positive, not a solve (`python/carnot/experiment_10013_planner_dedup_tiebreak.py:600-655`, `python/carnot/experiment_10013_planner_dedup_tiebreak.py:855-915`).
4. Primary metric: games with a **real first level-up** within both caps, paired against Current. Secondary: real actions to win, wasted actions after false positives, planner calls, wall time, and lost wins. Report the dynamics-control and search-ceiling arms separately; they cannot be pooled with induced dynamics. A promotion candidate needs at least one new game win, no lost baseline win, and no increase in total actions on shared winners. Treat ten games as a pilot, not a hidden-game estimate. Run a later held-out roster before changing defaults.

The cheap checks here set a low prior for a predicate-only fix: 5/38 missing, 11/38 constant, and 2/32 callable wrong rules rejected by real pre-win negatives (`results/raw/goal_induction_design_2026_09_25/per_engine.json:5-26`, `results/raw/goal_induction_design_2026_09_25/negative_only_veto.json:1-13`). The proposed loop has **not** been run. The prior 10013 expert-goal 5/10 to 6/10 search result is a control ceiling, not evidence that this new loop wins (`results/experiment_10013_planner_dedup_tiebreak.json:242-251`, `results/experiment_10013_planner_dedup_tiebreak.json:300-332`).

## 7. Open questions

- Which visible features distinguish goal objects from HUD counters when every action updates the HUD? The current sp80 target-fraction route was constant across 771 scored frontier nodes (`ops/verifier_gaps.md:3870-3917`).
- Can disagreement probes find a first win within an action-efficient budget, or will they only reject hypotheses? The existing active reward-machine evidence is fixture reachability, not a solve (`results/experiment_6387_arc_active_reward_machine_discriminator.json:1-125`).
- Can an engine reconstruct a terminal configuration from the last winning transition with enough independent dynamics accuracy to transfer? The post-level-up opening cannot serve as a positive, as the 0/6 transfer check shows (`results/raw/goal_induction_design_2026_09_25/single_positive_transfer.json:4-237`).
- How much of the remaining failure is dynamics or search? The positive-control study found several held-out windows where even correct grid-only dynamics cannot pass the unmasked exact gate, and the 10013 expert planner still misses short winning routes under 20,000 calls (`docs/research-notes/b2-positive-control-2026-09-23.md:33-72`, `docs/research-notes/goal-gradient-design-2026-09-25.md:7-11`).
