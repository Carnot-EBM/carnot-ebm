## Source A — Brown University News (2026-04-01/02 Lemley Lecture)

- URL: https://www.brown.edu/news/2026-04-01/yann-lecun-artificial-intelligence-pioneer
- Accessed: 2026-06-10

### A1. The agentic-prediction gap [1SRC] — THE quote for Carnot's verifier-validated acting
> "The result is that everybody these days in AI is talking about agentic systems — systems that
> can produce actions in the world — and almost none of those systems at the moment are capable of
> predicting the outcome… of their actions. It's a very bad way to produce an action… if you're
> not able to predict the consequences of it. In fact, it might be dangerous."

### A2. World-model definition [1SRC]
> "A world model is a predictive system that, given the current state of the world… and given an
> action that you imagine taking, [you can] predict the next state of the world that will result
> from this action or intervention. If you have such a world model that predicts what the world is
> going to be after you take an action, you can use that for planning."

### A3. On LLM-scaling investment [1SRC]
> "There's literally hundreds of billions invested in an industry that basically is counting on
> the fact that LLMs [are] going to reach human-level intelligence. It's complete BS."

### A4. On current systems [VERIFIED-2SRC for the final sentence]
> "AI sucks. We have systems that can manipulate language, and they fool us into thinking they are
> smart because they manipulate language. But in fact, they are completely helpless when it comes
> to the physical world."
