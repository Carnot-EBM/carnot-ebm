# V737 live capability mask panel

Exp8588 measures the existing opt-in supervisor mask on four exposed public
games. It uses the original Exp8536 wrapper qualification and Exp8587 publication
readiness. The fixed panel has seeds 11 and 22 and two applied modes, for 16
episodes. Each episode retains the 512-action and 120-second caps. Alternating
mode order, window 120, stall threshold 150, predicates and diversity behavior
remain those of the qualified wrapper.

The runner uses E3AgentPolicy through make_carnot_agent. Per-game adapters,
routes, solver artifacts, cached action-effect memory and source inspection
are withheld from the policy. No LLM is loaded or called. Common numeric
scoring is CPU work. Registry history is exposure information and never policy
input. Known levels receive zero new solve credit.

The primary result is
[experiment_8588_v737_arc_capability_panel.json](../../results/experiment_8588_v737_arc_capability_panel.json).
Its invocation work and frozen panel bind episode directories under
`results/raw/experiment_8588_v737_arc_capability_panel/`. Every episode retains
visible action/observation rows, cumulative induction attempts, direct supervisor
receipts and bounded child logs. Complete wall cost includes child startup and
shutdown. All seeds and four game-level pairs remain in the denominator.

Unavailable induction attempts and latch changes are distinct measurements.
Efficiency requires fewer actual unavailable attempts, no paired level regression
and no complete-wall increase. Readiness only qualifies measurement. SEVRA
motivates retaining progress-associated, regression-associated and unresolved
interventions. These labels describe observed associations; they do not estimate
unobserved causal counterfactual effects. Censored episodes and interventions
remain visible. Hidden-game performance, learning benefit and both generalization
scores remain unmeasured or zero as specified.

A qualified efficiency null closes this unchanged mask and panel. Refinement
requires at least three overlapping games and five resolved observations per
shared cell. The task neither trains an arm-selection policy nor changes the
default flag. Exp8529's source null, Exp8553's label rejection, Exp8559's cold-cost
null and all earlier quarantines remain intact. Ops and traceability reconciliation
belongs to the conductor for this invocation.

Observed collection on 2026-10-16 completed all 16 episodes and 8192 actions.
All episodes reached the action cap; no level progressed. Actual disabled
induction attempts fell from 16 in baseline to 8 with the mask. Complete wall
cost rose from 72.013856832 seconds to 73.240272943 seconds. The strict efficiency
gate therefore fails even though unavailable work decreased. No policy refinement
is supported by these unresolved interventions. Terminal qualification and exact
validation receipts are recorded in the primary artifact and its bound sidecar.
