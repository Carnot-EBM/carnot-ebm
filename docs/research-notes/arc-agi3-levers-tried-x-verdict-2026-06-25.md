# ARC-AGI-3 live-agent levers tried x verdict (as of 2026-06-25)

## TL;DR

The rapid-lever space is largely swept: across four families (exploration / candidate-generation, verifier-selection-pruner, world-model dynamics-engine induction, goal/`is_level_complete` induction) the overwhelming majority of tried levers returned `honest_null` or `modest_or_partial` (a real-but-non-level-banking efficiency/scaffolding win), with only a handful of `validated_positive` gates — none of which banked a NEW live level. The binding constraint is structural, not lever-shaped: the live deliverable runs an air-gapped weak 9B local inducer (Qwen3.5-9B-MTP) against hidden games, whereas the SOTA winner uses a strong coding-agent inducer to write+verify executable world models, and the L1-first-contact / L2-goal wall is "the winning prefix is never proposed" (generation), not "the verifier mis-selects" (selection). The genuinely-UNTRIED levers are the `unbuilt_mapped_only` SOTA ingestions (Epistemic-MCTS + causal-probe, MATM similarity-keyed retrieval, search-verifier/VerMCTS hierarchical search exp4478, affordance/navigation/llm-inducer/goal-acquisition mappings) (the one former `active_in_flight` build, exp4750 `.437 A2` structural-alignment detector fix, has LANDED `modest_or_partial`: it fixed the detector over-segmentation 42->2 but did NOT bank lp85 L2 — the residual `no_reachable_plan` shifted the binding wall from goal-quality onto the dynamics ENGINE).
