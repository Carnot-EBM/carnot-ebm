## Finite latent evidence

For each answer unit, score a bounded set of source windows plus a separate
missing-evidence state. Normalize energies over the finite state set. Marginalize
window location for the response label. Keep neutral and missing evidence as
different states: neutral means inspected source text neither supports nor
contradicts the answer; missing means no usable evidence was found within the
declared budget. Neither state proves falsehood. Weak response labels do not
identify sentence truth or a unique supporting span.

[EBT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02092) and
[ARM–EBM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15605) motivate learned energies.
Their results do not validate this small weakly supervised source model.
Compare it with logistic and pooled MLP controls using the same information.
Evaluate source erasure and swapped-source interventions.

## Isolate chunking from scoring

[HallDetect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05823) decomposes claims and checks
multiple source chunks. Its reported decomposition and chunking changes are
not a clean single-factor estimate. V672 holds the answer units, labels,
features, and budget fixed while changing one-window versus three-window
source segmentation. Preserve complete original clauses and count every
over-budget family as an abstention. This is a controlled local test, not a
HallDetect reproduction. [Enoki](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00581) is a
related source-alignment lead, not a supplied evaluator.

## Bound delayed feedback and cost

[Capacity-constrained delayed OCO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11711) shows
why pending feedback needs an explicit capacity. V672 records forecast time,
label arrival, pending budget, proposal, commit or rejection, and post-restart
retention. Its discrete acquisition mechanism inherits no convex regret
guarantee. A complete-static bank is the comparator; an empty bank is not.

Service efficiency includes host preparation, transfer, native call, durable
acknowledgement, and recovery. [FPGA/Ising co-design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15985)
and [Z1T](https://extropic.ai/writing/z1t) motivate full boundary accounting.
Vendor projections and solver-only timing do not prove local service speed.
