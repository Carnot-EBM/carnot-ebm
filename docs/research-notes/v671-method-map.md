# V671 method map: recover execution, then test useful learning

Date: 2026-09-26. Exp7699 is contract accounting. It runs CPU aggregation
and opens no scientific gate. The dated V671 review in `research-references.md`
is the method source. V670 made no experiment artifacts; its six exhausted
roots and eight gate skips are operational custody, not negative science.

| Method | V671 use | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Enoki](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00581) | Preserve text-anchored relations, arguments, modifiers, polarity, complete clauses and answer spans in Exp7700. | A deterministic checker is an adaptation, not Enoki's learned entailment verifier. Missing facts reduce recall. |
| [Structural failures and semantic gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23742) and [Thinking Before Constraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07525) | Score syntax, record addressing, exact binding and semantic support separately in Exp7702. | Valid JSON or a longer budget does not establish truth. |
| [Capacity-constrained delayed OCO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11711) | Bound pending feedback and admit constraints only after label release. | A discrete bank inherits no convex regret theorem. |
| [ADOWIP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25068) | Compare revealed-loss update allocation with fixed-period allocation under one proposal and admission budget in Exp7706. | Time-series proxy gains do not establish a Carnot gain; rejected proposals consume budget. |
| [FPGA/Ising co-design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15985) and [Extropic Z1T](https://extropic.ai/writing/z1t) | Charge source processing, orchestration, native calls and persistence in Exp7711. | Vendor estimates are not local board measurements. |

delta was established. This is an access failure, not evidence of no citations.

decoder or sampler, and generator training remain outside this milestone.
