# Carnot Position Paper Nexus
**Log Date:** 2026-05-16

This document serves as the nexus for the Carnot position paper, consolidating the architecture updates and strategic roadmap for Phase 3 and beyond.

## Abstract
Carnot is a dual-language (Rust + Python/JAX) Energy Based Model framework that enforces strict mathematical grounding via spec-anchored development. This position paper outlines the necessity of hardware-accelerated continuous-to-Ising transpilation and its integration into the verification pipeline.

## Architectural Updates
The architecture has been updated to reflect the position paper findings, specifically ensuring that hardware portability is recognized as a political requirement for decentralization.

## Conclusion
The path forward relies on the strict adherence to the energy function as the ground truth, avoiding simulated numbers in headline claims.
